## Notes on the tool

- `scripts/coverage-report.mjs` compares exact lowercased names, so **The Maestro / Maestro**,
  **The System AI / System AI**, **The Gate of the Feral Gods / Gate of the Feral Gods** and
  **Personal Spaces / Personal Space** are reported as gaps and are not. Strip a leading "the "
  and compare singular and plural before matching.
- It only scans the Characters and Items categories. The wiki also has NPCs (129 pages),
  Deities (56), Groups (59), Syndicate Organizations (33), Dungeon Locations (36), Dungeon
  Mechanics (52), Quests (38), Shows (25), Bosses (39), Races (110), Classes (32), Skills (105),
  Spells (95), Status Effects (68), Loot Boxes (77). A `--cat=` flag would make the tables above
  reproducible.
- The wiki's Images categories are fan art with named artists and no licence template; two
  official pieces are tagged fair use by the wiki. None of it is ours to republish.
